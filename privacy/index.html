Privacy Policy

ScrollSmack collects nothing, transmits nothing, and has no servers. The app has no network permission at all, so it is not technically capable of sending your data anywhere.

What the app stores

Everything ScrollSmack knows lives only on your device. The app's private storage holds:

  • Which apps you chose to track — the package names you ticked in the picker.
  • Your time limit — the number of minutes you selected.
  • Whether it is on, off or paused — including when a pause ends, when you turned it off (used to time reminders), and when you first turned it on.
  • Rating prompt counters — how many roasts you have dismissed, and how many times and when the app last asked for a rating.

ScrollSmack also keeps a small diagnostic log in its own app folder on your device's shared storage. It holds timestamps for events such as the timer starting or stopping, a pause, a reminder, a phone restart or a limit being crossed, along with the limit in minutes and whether the app's permissions are granted. It does not contain the names of the apps you use, and it is never sent anywhere.

That is the entire list. Deleting the app deletes it.

Android's Auto Backup is switched off for ScrollSmack, and both cloud backup and device-to-device transfer (Samsung Smart Switch and similar) are explicitly excluded. Your list of tracked apps never leaves the phone even when you migrate to a new one — the trade-off being that you re-pick your apps and limit after a factory reset or a new device.

What the app reads

  • Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) — ScrollSmack asks Android which app is currently in the foreground, roughly once a second, so it can measure how long you have been continuously using an app you chose to track. This reading never leaves your device, which app is on screen is never written to a file, and nothing is associated with an identity — the app has no accounts.
  • Installed apps — to show you a list to choose from, ScrollSmack asks Android for apps that have a launcher icon. This list is displayed to you and not recorded.

Google Play ratings

After you have used ScrollSmack for a few days, it may ask Google Play to show its standard rating prompt. Google Play displays that prompt and handles any rating or review you choose to submit, under Google's own privacy policy. ScrollSmack cannot see whether you rated it or what you wrote.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • No network requests of any kind.
  • No cloud backup and no device-to-device transfer of app data.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone, because no data is collected.

Children

ScrollSmack is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.
