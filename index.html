How it works

  • You choose which apps get watched. Nothing else is tracked.
  • The timer measures continuous use, not daily totals. Step away for 30 seconds and it pauses; longer and it resets.
  • Cross the limit and the roast takes over the screen. Tapping back doesn’t close it. You type the phrase — about four seconds — and that’s long enough to notice what you were doing.
  • It can’t stop you. Not a blocker, not parental control. The phrase is friction, not a lock.

Completely offline

ScrollSmack has no INTERNET permission. Not “we don’t collect data” — there is no network channel in the app, so there is nowhere for data to go. No accounts, no analytics, no ads. Cloud backup and device transfer are off too. A factory reset loses your settings and you re-pick your apps. Worth it.
