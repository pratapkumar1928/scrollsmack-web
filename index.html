System Architecture

How ScrollSmack Breaks Autopilot

Screen time limits fail because tapping "Ignore limit for today" takes zero cognitive effort. ScrollSmack replaces that instant bypass with four seconds of intentional friction.

STAGE 01 // SELECTION

Pick Your Targets

Choose the exact apps that eat your evenings — TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit. Only the apps you explicitly check get monitored. Nothing else is tracked.

STAGE 02 // MEASUREMENT

Continuous Limits

ScrollSmack tracks unbroken continuous rabbit holes, not daily utility totals. Set 5, 10, 15, or 30 minutes. Step away for 30s and the session pauses; step away longer and it resets.

STAGE 03 // OVERRIDE

The Roast Overlay

When the limit trips, ScrollSmack takes over the display with a full-screen roast. The Back button is locked down. Media behind it pauses. You must type the unlock phrase to dismiss it.
